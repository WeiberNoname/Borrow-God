# First audience playtest

Recruit 12–15 PC players who already enjoy action roguelites and experimenting with builds. Include keyboard/mouse and controller users, and several players who value character stories. English and Chinese-speaking recruitment is an initial audience hypothesis; the prototype currently has English text only.

Give each tester the entire `Builds/Windows` folder. Ask them to choose a god, complete a voyage, and decide whether to attempt another. Do not explain which route or tribute decision is preferred. Let the briefing and controls teach them. Keep their existing profile unless they voluntarily want to start fresh.

Record these observations:

- Time to first successful attack, dodge, and divine cast; whether help was needed.
- What the tester thinks Nera wants before choosing a route.
- Which route, relic, shrine gifts, and tribute decision they choose, with their reason.
- Whether shields, warning circles, and the combo finisher are understandable.
- Whether they use a relic's special behavior without coaching.
- Whether they speak to Nera after returning and understand Tomas's changed condition.
- Whether they start another voyage voluntarily.
- Any control, focus, readability, audio, or accessibility problem.

After play, ask: “What made the tribute worth paying or refusing?”, “Who were you trying to help?”, “What felt unfair or unclear?”, and “What would make you want another voyage?” Avoid asking leading questions about how meaningful the story was.

Judge patterns rather than treating this small sample as market proof. Repeated control failures or misunderstood consequences should be fixed before producing more content. Optional replay and remembered character stakes are useful signals of audience fit. The current short voyage does not establish the planned 20–30-minute commercial pacing.

This is the procedure for a future human playtest. No player study has been completed yet.
